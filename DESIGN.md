## Brand & Style

The design system embodies deep familial trust, unadulterated freshness, and heartfelt Bangladeshi hospitality. Rooted in the emotional equity of maternal blessing and everyday honesty, the visual language balances high-trust agricultural authenticity with modern digital grocery convenience. 

### Core Personality & Emotional Goals
- **Warm & Reassuring:** Prioritize transparent guarantees (e.g., zero-formalin, chemical-free testing, direct-from-orchard sourcing) through grounded microcopy and comforting visual hierarchy.
- **Appetizing & Natural:** Celebrate seasonal richness—from Rajshahi mangoes and Dinajpur litchis to handpicked citrus—leveraging warm cream backdrops that allow saturated fruit photography to dominate.
- **Affordable Premium:** Avoid detached luxury. The experience must feel polished, clean, and orderly while remaining unmistakably approachable to everyday households across Bangladesh.

### Design Movement: Tactile Organic Modern
The interface couples crisp digital commerce architecture with organic warmth: soft earthen containers, delicate leaf-tinted boundaries, rounded tactile surfaces, and rich harvest accents. High-contrast typography guarantees effortless scanning on mobile devices across varied network bandwidths.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from rural orchards, fertile soil, and ripe seasonal produce. Light mode is the default and mandatory operational canvas to ensure high legibility and an airy, hygienic food marketplace experience.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#154D2E`):** Deep Forest Green. Signifies uncompromised heritage, organic purity, primary navigation bars, dominant call-to-action buttons, and trust stamps.
- **Secondary (`#2E7D32`):** Fresh Leaf Green. Used for positive affirmation states, badge fills, stock status indicators, and active tab highlights.
- **Tertiary / Accent (`#E65100`):** Ripe Mango Golden Orange. Reserved for urgent or high-value actions: conversion triggers, promotional discount ribbons, pricing highlights, and seasonal cart alerts.
- **Neutral Surface Canvas (`#FBFAF6`):** Warm Cream. Replaces stark clinic-white base backgrounds to provide an earthy, reassuring canvas that reduces visual fatigue.
- **Elevated Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white reserved exclusively for cards, sheets, and modal containers to achieve crisp separation against the warm cream canvas.
- **Subtle Stroke & Hairline (`#E2DDD3`):** Warm natural outline that contains structures without creating harsh digital boxes.
- **Text & Hierarchy:**
  - **Headings & Body (`#19231C`):** Deep charcoal green for ultra-high contrast and readability.
  - **Muted Metadata (`#4A5C50`):** Balanced leafy gray for secondary weights, kilograms, and provenance metadata.

## Typography

The typography system operates on a dual-script hierarchy:

1. **Bengali Text Rendering:** Bengali copy relies on `Noto Sans Bengali` (or `Hind Siliguri`), configured with ample line-height multiples (minimum `1.45x` to `1.6x`) to accommodate ascenders, descenders, and complex ligatures without clipping.
2. **English & Numeric Rendering:** Latin content, weights (kg/gm), and currency figures (৳) rely on `Inter`, delivering clarity and strict tabular numeric alignment across product cards and checkouts.

### Usage Principles
- Avoid light font weights (`<400`) in Bengali copy to ensure consistent rendering on low-density mobile displays.
- Numerals in currency and product quantities must render consistently: keep Latin numerals in `Inter` semi-bold or Bengali numerals in matched geometric proportions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a standard **8px structural grid** (using a 4px half-step for compact metadata tags and icon lockups).

### Grid Architecture
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `16px` outer page margins and `12px` gutters. Product cards render as a dual-column grid (`calc(50% - 6px)`), optimizing one-handed tap targets for mobile shopping.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `24px` margins and `16px` gutters.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at `1200px` max-width with centered auto-margins and `24px` gutters. 

### Vertical Spacing & Flow
- Generous breathing room between content bands prevents perceptual clutter. Category rails and featured harvest selections maintain a minimum `32px` mobile and `48px` desktop vertical separation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **ambient, warmth-tinted shadows** rather than harsh cold black drop shadows. Surfaces feel raised like wooden produce crates and clean paper cartons.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Flat Base (Level 0):** Background canvas (`#FBFAF6`) with zero shadow.
- **Card Rest (Level 1):** Product cards, input containers, and category tiles use a subtle dual-layer shadow:
  `0 2px 4px rgba(21, 77, 46, 0.03), 0 6px 16px rgba(25, 35, 28, 0.05)` coupled with a `1px solid #E2DDD3` border.
- **Hover & Interactive (Level 2):** Elevated cards on desktop hover or tap-hold:
  `0 8px 24px rgba(21, 77, 46, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(25, 35, 28, 0.04)`. Border shifts dynamically to `#2E7D32` at 30% opacity.
- **Sticky Bars & Floating Elements (Level 3):** Bottom navigation, checkout cart drawers, and floating WhatsApp/Phone hotlines:
  `0 -4px 20px rgba(25, 35, 28, 0.08)`.
- **Modals & Dialogs (Level 4):** Formalin-test certificates, location selectors, and seasonal notifications:
  `0 16px 40px rgba(21, 77, 46, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a reassuring, friendly curvature. Standard elements apply `roundedness: 2` (`0.5rem` / `8px` default radius), while prominent customer-facing surfaces utilize generous roundedness to convey softness and approachability.

### Curvature Rules
- **Product Cards & Surfaces (`rounded-2xl` / `16px`):** Large, inviting corner radii reflect organic shapes and tactile comfort.
- **Buttons & Search Bars (`rounded-xl` / `12px` or Full Pill):** Primary transactional buttons maintain a structured `12px` radius; quick-filter chips and cart counters transition to full pill (`rounded-full`).
- **Image Frames:** Fruit hero images sit inside matched `14px` internal radii, preventing visual overlap with card borders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("এখনই কিনুন" / "অর্ডার করুন"):** Forest Green background (`#154D2E`), text in `#FFFFFF`, `12px` border radius, `14px/20px` bold typography. Vertical padding `12px`, horizontal padding `20px`. Hover shifts to `#1E653D`. Active press applies a subtle `scale(0.98)` spring animation.
- **Accent Action ("কার্টে যোগ করুন"):** Warm Mango Orange (`#E65100`), white text, reserved for cart conversion and limited seasonal pre-orders.
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Surface white with `1.5px` border in `#E2DDD3`, text in `#154D2E`.

### Cards (Product Card)
- **Container:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) surface with `16px` border-radius, `1px solid #E2DDD3` border, and Level 1 elevation.
- **Visual Zone:** Top aspect-ratio container (`1:1`) displaying fruit with neutral or soft warm environmental staging.
- **Trust Badges:** Absolute positioning at top-left: e.g., "১০০% কেমিক্যাল মুক্ত" (100% Chemical Free) in small leaf green pill badges (`#E8F5E9` bg, `#154D2E` text).
- **Price Block:** Large bold numerals in `Inter` (`#154D2E`), unit weight indicator in Bengali (`প্রতি কেজি`) in `#4A5C50`.

### Chips & Filters
- Compact `rounded-full` pills for quick seasonal categories (e.g., 'আমের মৌসুম', 'মাল্টা ও কমলা', 'মৌসুমি ড্রাগন').
- Inactive: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2DDD3`, text `#4A5C50`.
- Active: Background `#154D2E`, border `1px solid #154D2E`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Address & Phone Input:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E2DDD3`, radius `10px`, padding `12px 16px`. Focus state shifts border color to `#154D2E` with a delicate `0 0 0 3px rgba(21, 77, 46, 0.12)` halo ring.
- **Phone Prefix:** Pre-filled `+880` lockup in muted tone with divider separator.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Selection accents styled in `#154D2E` with white check indicators. Touch targets padded to a minimum of `44px × 44px` for frictionless mobile ordering.

### Trust & Quality Stamp Component
- A dedicated horizontal verification strip across key pages: displays formal laboratory testing badges, "ক্যাশ অন ডেলিভারি" (Cash on Delivery), and "পছন্দ না হলে তাৎক্ষণিক রিটার্ন" (Instant Return if Not Satisfied) backed by subtle leaf iconography.
